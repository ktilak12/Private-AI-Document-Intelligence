# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT & SEPARATION COVENANT

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Parties:** PAIDI Enterprise Holdings Inc. ("Company") and Executive Employee ("Executive")  
**Jurisdiction:** State of Delaware, United States  

## Section 1: Position and Duties
The Executive shall serve as Principal AI Research Director, reporting directly to the Chief Executive Officer and the Board of Directors. Executive shall oversee high-assurance zero-leakage neural search architectures.

## Section 2: Compensation and Equity Vesting
1. **Base Salary:** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $340,000 USD, payable semi-monthly.
2. **Equity Acceleration:** In the event of a Change of Control accompanied by involuntary termination without Cause, one hundred percent (100%) of unvested equity options shall immediately vest and become exercisable under Single-Trigger Acceleration protocols.

## Section 8: Termination and Separation Covenant
### §8.1 Termination for Cause
The Company may terminate Executive's employment immediately for Cause upon written notice specifying criminal misconduct, material breach of fiduciary duty, or willful disclosure of confidential enclave keys.

### §8.2 Termination Without Cause & Notice Period
Either party may terminate this Agreement without Cause upon providing a mandatory **thirty (30) calendar days prior written notice**. 
In the event the Company terminates Executive without Cause:
1. **Severance Pay:** Executive shall receive twelve (12) months of continuing base salary, paid in accordance with standard payroll cycles.
2. **COBRA Healthcare:** The Company shall subsidize healthcare coverage premiums under COBRA for up to twelve (12) months.
3. **Indemnity Obligations:** The Company shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Executive against all civil actions, liabilities, or regulatory inquiries arising out of Executive's good-faith performance of duties.

## Section 14: Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality
Executive acknowledges that proprietary models, embedding algorithms, cryptographic tenant keys, and source code are Trade Secrets. Non-compete and non-solicitation restrictions shall remain in effect for eighteen (18) months following separation.
